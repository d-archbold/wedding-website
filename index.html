Information

The Venue

For the celebration, please head to the second floor. Before the speeches, we encourage you to have a wander and explore the museum and grounds. Your entrance to the museum is included as our guest and there will be a Vivienne Westwood exhibition on the first floor. Please keep drinks on the second floor only and note that the museum is open to the public until 5pm.

The Céilí

A céilí is a traditional Irish group dance. Don't worry if you don't know the moves, a "caller" will be there to teach everyone the steps! It's great fun, so please bring sensible shoes to dance the night away.

Dress Code

Cocktail. Colourful clothing encouraged; please avoid all white or black.

Gifting

No gifts are necessary; we appreciate people travelling and being with us to celebrate. Should you wish to give something, we would appreciate a donation to our honeymoon to New Zealand.

Parking and Access

Free parking is available at the Bowes Museum. Lifts are available.
